About Us

A place built from a dream of warm, easy hospitality.

The story of Hiraya Hotel

Hiraya Hotel began with a simple idea: a place where travelers feel looked after from the moment they arrive. We built it with comfortable rooms, quiet spaces, and a team that treats every guest like family.

Today we welcome couples, families, and groups of friends who want to rest, celebrate, or simply get away. Our staff are trained to anticipate what you need, so you can focus on enjoying your stay.

In Filipino, hiraya means the fruit of imagination: a dream, a vision. It is the promise behind our name.

What we care about

Warm hospitality

We greet you by name, remember what you like, and fix problems quickly and quietly.

Comfort and cleanliness

Every room is cleaned to a high standard, with soft bedding, strong air conditioning, and fast Wi-Fi.

Local pride

We hire from our community and serve local dishes, so your stay supports the people around us.
